My story

I started my career in Yangon, in business development at AYA SOMPO Insurance, managing client accounts and learning how to turn conversations into results. Before that, I led international programmes as a volunteer with AIESEC, working with teams in Thailand and Vietnam.

images/story-1.jpg

In 2025 I moved to Dubai to join The Customization Group in e-commerce operations, running marketplace accounts for a multi-brand photo-print business. Seeing the business from the inside, from orders and listings to customer complaints, taught me what makes people buy and what makes them hesitate.

images/story-2.jpg

In 2026 I moved into a creative role. Since then I’ve been producing AI-powered ads, landing pages and product imagery for stores across the US, UK and Europe, and I taught myself enough coding to build both the pages and the AI systems behind my work.

What drives me

I’m driven by curiosity, the desire to keep improving, and the freedom to create the future I envision for myself.

I love exploring unfamiliar things, challenging what I already know and discovering what I’m capable of along the way. I’m always looking for chances to learn, grow and push past my current limits.

Ultimately, I want to build a life where I’m free to explore possibilities, pursue what excites me, and create opportunities rather than wait for them.
