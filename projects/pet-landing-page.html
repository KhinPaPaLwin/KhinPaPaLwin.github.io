Pet use-case campaign

In an A/B test against the existing landing page, the pet landing page I designed and built delivered 41% higher ROAS from the same ads.

Market
United States
Channel
Meta ads
Role
End to end
landing page screenshot
ad video
ad static

The brief

Test whether a use-case campaign works better than our general setup: a landing page and creatives made specifically for one use case, pet portraits on canvas, shown to pet owners.

What I did

I owned the whole pipeline, from research to live page. I benchmarked competitors’ landing pages to see how they sell pet portraits, then designed the page layout and built it in HTML and CSS with Claude Code. I generated the images on the page and produced the ad creatives, both statics and video, so the ad and the page told one consistent story.

The result

The campaign ran as an A/B test. Both versions used the same ads, the same audience, a similar budget and the same dates; only the landing page differed. In the US, the new pet page delivered 41% higher ROAS than the existing page.

What I learned

Visitors were most interested in size, price and savings. They engaged most with the size and price table and the discount on additional canvases, which showed that people shopping for a canvas want to know what size to choose, what it costs and how much they can save.

Those same elements were where we found dead clicks: visitors tapping discount badges and pricing tables that weren’t links. We made them clickable, turning those taps into a path to purchase.
