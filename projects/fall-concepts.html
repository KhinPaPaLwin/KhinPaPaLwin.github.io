Fall campaign

By refining summer’s winning concepts for fall, my creatives delivered 2.5× the ROAS and around 45% higher click-through rate than three identically set-up campaigns running in the same weeks.

Markets
US and Europe
Channel
Meta ads
Role
Concept and AI production
fall static
fall video
fall static, second market

The brief

Bring the winning concepts from our summer campaigns into fall. The images needed to reflect the people who make up most of our customers, with a light touch of Halloween so the ads could keep running through the whole season instead of expiring after a few weeks.

What I did

I turned the brief into a wide set of creative concepts and tested them across regions. For statics, I refined the summer winners with a realistic, everyday look: real lived-in rooms, natural light and the canvas as the hero of each shot. For video, I produced a mix of formats, from UGC-style clips to a home tour with Halloween décor, so we could learn which style worked best in each market.

The result

Compared with campaigns that had the same targeting and optimisation and ran in the same weeks, the Fall campaign delivered 2.5× the ROAS and around 45% higher click-through rate, and it became the US account’s lead campaign. The ROAS also benefited from budget and bidding changes made by the paid social team towards the end of the campaign, so it’s a shared result. The click-through rate is the clearest sign of the creatives’ part in it.
