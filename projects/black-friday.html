Black Friday campaign

When my Black Friday creatives replaced the fall ads, the same campaigns across six European stores returned 42% higher ROAS at almost the same daily budget.

Markets
US and Europe
Channel
Meta ads
Role
Concept and AI production
Black Friday static
Black Friday UGC video
dark neon-style static

The brief

Go straight to selling: offer-led ads for canvas only, running from mid-November to the end of the month, with a few early Christmas-gift touches to lead into the holiday season.

What I did

I produced two looks for the Black Friday statics: a bright lifestyle set showing canvases in real homes with an early Christmas touch, and darker neon-style ads in the typical Black Friday aesthetic. For video, I made a mix of UGC-style and simple product-focused videos.

The result

From mid-November, the Black Friday ads replaced the fall creatives inside the existing campaigns, with the same targeting and almost the same daily budget. Across six European stores, ROAS rose 42% and click-through rate 17%, and every store improved. In the US, Meta moved almost the entire budget onto the new ads, and ROAS held steady even as daily spend rose by about 70%.
