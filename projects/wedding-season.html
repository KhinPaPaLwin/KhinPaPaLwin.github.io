Wedding campaign

My wedding creatives for the UK and German stores took about a fifth of Meta spend in Europe during the campaign and returned 25% higher ROAS than the rest of the account over the same period.

Markets
US, UK, Germany
Products
5
Role
Landing page imagery and ads
wedding static
wedding UGC video
wedding landing page

The brief

Test a use-case campaign for weddings: a themed landing page and creatives made for newly married couples and the people buying gifts for them, across the US, UK and German stores. The campaign covered five products: canvas, acrylic print, photo mug, photobook and MixPix.

What I did

I created the wedding-themed images for the landing pages in every region, plus static and UGC video ads for each product in each region. The photobook and MixPix were the hardest. In the photobook videos, the couple had to stay consistent as the book opened and the pages turned. MixPix was shown as a gallery wall of 12–16 tiles, so the people had to stay consistent across every tile, and the tiles had to stay in order through every camera movement.

The result

The campaigns scaled to a large share of the European budget while outperforming the rest of the account in the same months. In the US, results were in line with the account average, which is why the UK and Germany are the headline here.
